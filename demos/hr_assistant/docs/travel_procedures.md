# Acme Corp – Travel Booking Procedures and Vendor List
**Document Owner: Finance & Admin | Last Updated: March 2024**

---

## Approved Travel Booking Process

### Step 1 – Raise a Travel Request
1. Log into the HR/Finance Portal at `portal.acmecorp.internal`.
2. Navigate to **Travel → New Request**.
3. Fill in: destination, travel dates, purpose, estimated cost.
4. Manager approval is required before booking.

### Step 2 – Book via Approved Channels
All travel must be booked through **TravelSmart** (Acme's approved travel management company) or the portal's integrated booking tool. Direct bookings are only permitted when the destination is not served by TravelSmart, and only with prior Finance approval.

**TravelSmart contact:** travelsmart@acmecorp.internal | +91-22-4444-5555

### Step 3 – Submit Expenses
See HR Policy Manual Section 2.4 for submission timelines and receipt requirements.

## Domestic Flight Guidelines

| Route Type | Permitted Class | Max Advance Booking |
|------------|----------------|----------------------|
| < 2 hours | Economy only | 7 days |
| 2–5 hours | Economy | 14 days |
| > 5 hours | Economy (Business with approval) | 30 days |

Acme has a corporate agreement with **IndiGo Airlines** for domestic routes. Preferred booking discounts of 8–12% apply when booked through TravelSmart.

## International Flight Guidelines

Preferred carriers: **Air India**, **Emirates**, **Singapore Airlines** (for Asian destinations); **Lufthansa**, **British Airways** (for European destinations).

For flights over 8 hours, Business Class is permitted per HR Policy Manual Section 2.3. Employees must still select the lowest available Business Class fare.

## Ground Transport

- **Preferred cab vendor:** Acme tie-up with **Meru Cabs** (booking via portal). No cash payments.
- **Personal vehicle:** ₹8 per km (declare vehicle registration at first claim).
- **Auto-rickshaw/metro:** Actual cost, receipt required above ₹200.
- **Rental cars:** Only for travel to locations not served by cabs, with prior approval. Max ₹2,500/day.

---

## Foreign Currency and Forex

For international travel, employees may:
1. Draw a **forex card** from HDFC Bank (Acme's bank partner) through the portal. Pre-loaded with the approved per diem amount.
2. Submit original forex receipts or bank statements for any out-of-pocket expenses in foreign currency.

Reimbursements for foreign currency are at the **RBI reference rate** on the date of expenditure. Personal forex conversion charges are not reimbursable.

---

## Cancelled and Changed Bookings

- Cancellations due to **company reasons** (project change, manager cancellation): fully reimbursed including cancellation fees.
- Cancellations due to **personal reasons**: employee bears all cancellation/change fees. Approval required from HR to categorize a cancellation as company-initiated.
