# Acme Corp – Employee HR Policy Manual (Shore Staff)
**Version 4.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024**

---

## 1. Leave Policies

### 1.1 Annual Leave
Shore staff are entitled to **24 days** of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 2 days per month. Unused leave can be carried forward for a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. Any balance exceeding 10 days will be forfeited on January 1st.

**How to apply:** Submit a leave request via the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for leaves of 3 days or fewer, and at least 15 working days for leaves exceeding 3 days.

**Approval authority:** Line manager for up to 5 days; Department Head for 6–14 days; HR Director for 15 days or more.

### 1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to **12 days** of paid sick leave per year. For absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**, a medical certificate from a registered physician is mandatory. Sick leave does not carry forward.

Sick leave during annual leave: If an employee falls ill during annual leave and can provide a medical certificate, those sick days will be reclassified as sick leave and annual leave will be restored.

### 1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers.
- **Paternity leave:** 4 weeks fully paid, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Adoption leave:** Same as maternity leave for the primary adopting caregiver.

### 1.4 Emergency Leave
Up to **3 days** per occurrence (maximum 6 days per year) for the death or serious illness of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Documentation required within 5 working days.

### 1.5 Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to **90 days** may be granted at the discretion of the HR Director for personal reasons (education, travel, family care). Pension contributions are suspended during unpaid leave.

## 2. Travel and Reimbursement Policy

### 2.1 Eligibility
All shore staff traveling on company business are eligible for reimbursement as outlined below.

### 2.2 Domestic Travel
- **Per diem (meals + incidentals):** ₹1,500 per day for metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad); ₹1,000 per day for other cities.
- **Hotel accommodation:** Actual cost up to ₹5,000 per night (metro) or ₹3,500 (other cities). Pre-approval required for bookings above these limits.
- **Local transport:** Actual cost (taxi/cab). Personal vehicles: ₹8 per km.

### 2.3 International Travel
- **Per diem (meals + incidentals):** USD 50 per day (Asia); USD 75 per day (Europe, Americas, Australia).
- **Hotel accommodation:** Actual cost up to USD 150 per night (Asia); USD 200 per night (elsewhere). Pre-approval required for bookings above these limits.
- **Business class air travel:** Permitted for flights exceeding **8 hours** in duration, or for employees at Director level and above.
- **Economy class:** All other flights. Upgrades at personal expense.

### 2.4 Expense Submission
- Expenses must be submitted within **21 days** of return from travel.
- All expenses above ₹500 (domestic) or USD 20 (international) require original receipts.
- Submit via the HR portal under "Expense Claims."
- Reimbursements are processed within **10 working days** of approved submission.

### 2.5 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are NOT reimbursable: personal entertainment, alcohol, minibar charges, fines/penalties, personal travel add-ons, and upgrades.

## 3. Code of Conduct

### 3.1 Core Principles
All employees are expected to:
1. Act with **integrity and honesty** at all times.
2. Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with **respect and dignity**.
3. Avoid **conflicts of interest** and disclose any potential conflicts to their manager and HR.
4. Protect **confidential information** of the company and clients.
5. Comply with all **applicable laws and regulations**.

### 3.2 Workplace Harassment and Discrimination
Acme Corp maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** for harassment (including sexual harassment) and discrimination based on race, gender, religion, nationality, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

Complaints must be reported to HR or the Ethics Helpline within 30 days. Investigations will be completed within 45 days. Retaliation against complainants is prohibited and is itself a disciplinary offense.

### 3.3 Social Media Policy
Employees must not post confidential company information, client data, or disparaging content about the company, its employees, or clients on social media. Personal views expressed online must be clearly distinguished as personal and not company positions.

### 3.4 Gifts and Hospitality
Employees may accept gifts of **nominal value only** (up to ₹2,000 or equivalent). All gifts above this threshold must be declared to HR and either returned or donated. Offering gifts to clients or government officials in excess of this limit is prohibited.

### 3.5 Disciplinary Process
Violations of the Code of Conduct are handled through a structured process:
1. **Verbal warning** (minor first offence)
2. **Written warning** (repeat minor offence or single moderate offence)
3. **Final written warning** (serious offence)
4. **Termination** (gross misconduct or repeated violations)

Gross misconduct (e.g., fraud, assault, data theft) may result in immediate termination without prior warning.

## 4. Performance Management

### 4.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted **bi-annually**: mid-year review in July and annual review in January. The annual review determines merit increases and bonus eligibility.

### 4.2 Rating Scale
| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | Significantly exceeds all expectations |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | Consistently exceeds most expectations |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | Fully meets all role requirements |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | Partially meets expectations; improvement plan required |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | Fails to meet expectations; termination may result |

### 4.3 Bonus
Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for a performance bonus of **5–15% of annual salary**. Bonuses are paid in February following the annual review. Employees joining after October 1st are not eligible for that year's bonus.

## 5. Benefits

### 5.1 Health Insurance
Company-paid health insurance covers the employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children. Coverage: hospitalization up to ₹5 lakh/year (employee), ₹3 lakh/year (dependents). Dental and optical reimbursements: ₹10,000/year combined.

### 5.2 Provident Fund (PF)
Both employee and employer contribute **12% of basic salary** to the Employees' Provident Fund. Employees may voluntarily contribute up to 100% of basic salary.

### 5.3 Gratuity
Employees completing 5 or more years of continuous service are eligible for gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.

### 5.4 Flexible Working
Shore staff may apply for flexible working arrangements (flexible hours or partial remote work) after completing **6 months** of employment. Approval is at the manager's discretion based on role requirements.

---

## 6. Data Privacy and IT Usage

Employees may use company IT resources only for legitimate business purposes. Downloading unauthorized software, accessing inappropriate websites, or transmitting company data to personal devices without approval is prohibited. All company devices are subject to monitoring. Employees have no expectation of privacy on company systems.
