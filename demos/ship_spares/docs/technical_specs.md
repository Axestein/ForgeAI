# Maritime Technical Specifications – Vessel Equipment Standards
**Document: TechSpec-003 | Issued: January 2024 | Fleet: Acme Maritime Fleet**

---

## 1. Propulsion System Standards

### 1.1 Propeller Shaft Requirements
- Material: 316L stainless steel for saltwater applications; carbon steel acceptable for freshwater only.
- Surface finish: Ra ≤ 1.6 µm at bearing areas.
- Straightness tolerance: ≤ 0.05mm per meter of shaft length.
- Annual inspection mandatory; replace every **10 years** or when wear exceeds 2% of nominal diameter.

### 1.2 Propeller Selection Criteria
| Vessel HP | Recommended Propeller Diameter | Recommended Pitch Ratio |
|-----------|-------------------------------|------------------------|
| < 200 HP | 18–20 inch | 1.0–1.1 |
| 200–500 HP | 22–26 inch | 0.9–1.0 |
| > 500 HP | 28 inch+ | Custom design required |

4-blade propellers recommended for vessels operating in manoeuvring-intensive environments. 3-blade for open-ocean transit vessels.

### 1.3 Shaft Seals
Rubber lip seals (Type-A): Replace every **3 years** or **6,000 operating hours**.
Mechanical seals (Type-B): Replace every **5 years** or at next scheduled dry-dock.

## 2. Electrical System Standards

### 2.1 Power Supply
- Main ship supply: 440V 3-phase 60Hz (ocean vessels), 230V single phase 50Hz (coastal).
- Shore power connection: 32A 3-phase via **IEC 60309** approved inlet.
- Emergency power: Dedicated emergency battery bank; must sustain navigation lights, bilge pumps, and emergency radio for minimum **12 hours**.

### 2.2 Battery Maintenance Schedule
| Battery Type | Inspection | Replacement |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Start Battery | Monthly | Every 3 years |
| Service AGM | Quarterly | Every 5 years |
| Emergency Bank | Monthly | Every 4 years |

### 2.3 Navigation Lights
All navigation lights must comply with **COLREGS 1972 (as amended)**. LED lights are accepted if certified. Full light set inspection: every 12 months before the vessel's annual survey.

## 3. Hull Maintenance Schedule

### 3.1 Anti-fouling
- Tropical/subtropical routes: Reapply every **18 months** or at dry-dock.
- Temperate routes: Reapply every **24 months**.
- Two coats minimum. Surface must be blasted to SA 2.5 before application.

### 3.2 Zinc Anodes
Inspect every **6 months**. Replace when consumed to **50% of original mass**. For vessels in tropical waters, increase inspection to every **3 months**.

### 3.3 Hull Survey Schedule (Classification Society)
| Survey Type | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Annual Survey | Every 12 months |
| Intermediate Survey | Every 2.5 years |
| Special/Dry-dock Survey | Every 5 years |

## 4. Engine Maintenance Intervals

| Maintenance Item | Interval |
|-----------------|---------|
| Engine oil change | Every 500 operating hours or 12 months |
| Fuel filter replacement | Every 250 hours |
| Coolant change | Every 2 years |
| Heat exchanger cleaning | Every 1,000 hours |
| Impeller replacement | Every 500 hours or annually |
| Main engine overhaul | Every 10,000 hours |

Maintain engine logs in ProcureShip system. Deviations from maintenance intervals require Chief Engineer sign-off.

## 5. Navigation Equipment Calibration

| Equipment | Calibration Interval | Authority |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------|
| GPS/Chart Plotter | No calibration required (satellite-based) | — |
| Magnetic Compass | Annual deviation card update | Qualified adjuster |
| Radar | Annual performance check | Approved service agent |
| AIS Transponder | Annual functional test | MRCC or approved agent |
| VHF Radio | Annual test, 5-year type approval renewal | Flag State |
