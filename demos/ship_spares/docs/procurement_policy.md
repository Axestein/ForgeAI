## 3. Ordering Process

### 3.1 Standard Orders
1. Chief Engineer submits **Material Requisition (MR)** via procurement system (ProcureShip portal).
2. Shore Superintendent validates technical specs.
3. PO raised by Fleet Management Office.
4. Supplier confirms availability and delivery date within **24 hours**.
5. Parts shipped to designated port agent.

### 3.2 Urgent/Emergency Orders
For breakdown situations:
1. Chief Engineer contacts Fleet Manager by phone/satellite.
2. Verbal approval granted; MR to follow within 2 hours.
3. Supplier contacted directly for emergency airfreight if needed.
4. Emergency airfreight surcharge (typically 25–40% of part cost) pre-approved for breakdown situations.

### 3.3 Port Agent Delivery
All spares deliveries must be coordinated with the port agent at the vessel's next port of call. Minimum **5 days notice** required for port agent arrangement. For remote ports, 10 days notice required.

## 5. Quality Standards and Inspection

- All parts must conform to **IMO, SOLAS, and Flag State regulations**.
- Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts preferred. Equivalent (non-OEM) parts may be used only with technical approval from the fleet's Classification Society.
- Incoming inspection required for:
  - Safety equipment (life rafts, EPIRBs, immersion suits): Third-party survey required.
  - Navigation electronics: Sea trial verification.
  - Propulsion components: Dimensional inspection on receipt.

Certificates required with delivery:
- Material Test Certificates (MTCs) for steel/metal parts
- Class-approved certificates for safety-critical parts
- Manufacturer's Declaration of Conformity for electrical equipment

---

## 6. Warranty and Returns

- Standard warranty: **12 months** from date of installation or **18 months** from date of delivery, whichever comes first.
- Defective parts must be reported within **30 days** of discovery.
- Return authorization (RMA) required from supplier before shipping defective parts back.
- Shipping costs for warranty returns: supplier's responsibility.

---

## 7. Inventory Management

Minimum stock levels are defined for critical spares (see **Critical Spares List CSL-2024**). The Chief Engineer must report any stock below minimum monthly. Automatic reorder is triggered when stock falls below **50% of minimum**.

Annual inventory count: last week of December. Discrepancies > 5% require an investigation report.

---

## 8. Prohibited Procurement Practices

- Splitting orders to avoid approval thresholds is prohibited.
- Personal procurement through company accounts is prohibited.
- Accepting kickbacks or gifts from suppliers is prohibited (see Code of Conduct).
- Procuring from blacklisted suppliers is prohibited. Blacklist updated quarterly by Fleet Management.
